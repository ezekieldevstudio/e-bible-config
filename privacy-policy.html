BibleLeaf Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Developer: Ezekiel Dev Studio · Android package: com.ezekiel.ebible

BibleLeaf is an offline Bible reader. No BibleLeaf account, login, church membership, payment, or developer-operated user-data server is provided.

Reading information and photos

Reading position, bookmarks, display preferences, selected voice and cover design are stored on your device. When you choose a photo, a copy is saved in the app's private storage for the cover; the original is not modified. BibleLeaf does not upload these records or photos to a developer server. Android device backup or device transfer may include app data according to your system settings (backup is enabled). Removing app data or uninstalling may remove local records and copied photos; system backups are managed separately by Android.

Advertising and consent

A Google AdMob banner appears only on the main menu. Reading, search and bookmarks do not display ads. Google Mobile Ads and its User Messaging Platform may process IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), app interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK transfers use TLS. Consent is requested where applicable before ads may be requested. Where required, an advertising privacy settings button is available on the menu to revise choices. Android advertising ID controls are in device settings. Ads may fail without preventing Bible reading.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. BibleLeaf has no separate analytics SDK or user registration service. Advertising SDK processing and retention are governed by Google's policies and applicable consent.

Read aloud and external pages

Read aloud uses the Android text-to-speech engine and selected voice. The selected Bible text is passed to that engine. Some engines or voices require network processing; offline availability and provider data handling depend on your chosen engine. BibleLeaf does not use an AI voice server. Opening external policy links launches your browser, whose own privacy policy applies.

Your choices

You can change reading preferences, remove bookmarks, replace your cover and adjust TTS voices within the app. Device settings provide app data removal, permissions, backup and advertising controls. There is no BibleLeaf account to delete. We do not intentionally request personal information from children. Contact us with privacy concerns; do not send sensitive data unnecessarily.

Contact and updates

Privacy inquiries: ezekieldevstudio@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when app behavior changes; the effective date will be revised.
